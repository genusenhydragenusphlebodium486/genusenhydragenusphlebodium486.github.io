Keep the application open while you use the camera. It is the bridge between the camera and your video programs, so closing it will make the camera disappear from those programs. If you want to stop using the camera, simply close the application and the camera is released back to your computer.

🌫️ About the Background Blur

The background blur, sometimes called bokeh, is the standout feature. The PS5 HD Camera contains two lenses that see the scene from slightly different positions—similar to how your two eyes work. The application compares both images, works out roughly how far away each part of the scene is, and then softens everything behind you while leaving you crisp and clear. The result looks like you are sitting in front of a professional camera with a wide aperture lens, even if there is a kitchen, a laundry basket, or a pile of cables behind you.

Good, even lighting in front of you gives the cleanest result. A window or lamp facing you helps the camera tell you apart from your surroundings. If you move around a lot, you may notice the blur adjusting slightly as it keeps track of what is in front and what is behind, which is normal behaviour for this kind of depth-based effect.

💻 What You Need

  • A PlayStation 5 HD Camera, model CFI-ZEY1, with its original cable.
  • A computer running Windows 11 or a modern Linux distribution. macOS works as well, but the background blur is not available there.
  • A free USB port, preferably a fast one, connected directly to the computer.
  • On Linux, a Vulkan-capable graphics card for the background blur feature.
  • About a few hundred megabytes of free disk space for the application itself.

🐧 Notes for Linux Users

On Linux the application presents the camera as a virtual video device, so any program that can open a normal camera will find it. Background blur runs on your graphics card through Vulkan, which keeps the effect smooth even on modest hardware. Make sure your graphics drivers are installed and up to date, then open the application and follow the same steps as on Windows. If you prefer a minimal setup, the application can also run without the blur if your graphics card does not support Vulkan.

🍎 Notes for macOS Users

On macOS the camera works as a regular webcam at its normal resolution, so you can use it for calls and recordings. The background blur effect is not available on macOS, so you will see the full, unblurred image. When you first open the application, macOS may ask for permission to access your camera—allow it, otherwise the video feed will not appear.
